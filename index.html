Left 4 Dead survivor guide

PC controls, the five special infected, and how to shove. Press any key on your keyboard while on the controls tab to see its action light up.

Things that are not keys

When you are incapacitated you can still fire your pistol with left click. If you hang from a ledge, a teammate has to pull you up with E. Reviving takes a few seconds, so clear the area first.

Playing Infected in Versus changes the controls per class. Left click is the main attack for most, and right click is a secondary ability such as the Tank throwing a rock. Check the Keyboard and Mouse options in the game for every exact binding, and rebind anything you like.

Two rules for every fight

  • Listen for the audio cues. Each special infected has its own sound, and it often warns you before the attack.
  • Stay together as a team. Almost every special infected is much worse against a lone survivor.

What shoving does

  • It works with any weapon, even when your gun is empty, so it is your emergency button when ammo runs out.
  • It pushes back common infected and staggers special infected, which buys you time to shoot them or get away.
  • Shoving has a stamina limit. Spam it and your shove weakens, then stops for a moment, so use short bursts.
  • Shoving a Hunter mid leap can knock him off course. Shoving a Smoker or Boomer at close range gives you space to finish them.
  • When a teammate is pinned by a Hunter or choked by a Smoker, shoving the infected is the fastest way to free them.
  • With a grenade or melee style item, right click still shoves. The first aid kit uses right click to heal a teammate, so switch slots first if you want to shove.
